simple. you knew tic-tac-toe? then you knew like 90% of the game.

except, it can:
- move your stones (or dots if you prefer to call it that way)
- you can move your stones. vertically. horizontally. or diagonally.

how to win?? cmon you knew tic-tac-toe right? thats how you win. its literally almost same.
